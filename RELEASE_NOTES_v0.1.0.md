# AirGlass Windows v0.1.0

**Mirror your computer screen to your phone over Wi-Fi**

---

## ⚡ Quick Start

### 1. Download
Get `AirGlass.exe` from this release page.

### 2. Run
Double-click the exe on Windows 10+.

### 3. Start Mirroring
Type: `start`

A QR code appears.

### 4. Connect Your Phone
1. Open Camera on iPhone
2. Point at the QR code
3. Tap the notification
4. Your screen appears in Safari!

### 5. Stop
Type: `stop` when done.

**Done!** No installation, no setup.

---

## What You Need

**Your Computer:**
- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- ~200 MB disk space

**Your Phone:**
- iPhone 6 or newer
- iOS 11 or newer
- Same Wi-Fi network

---

## Features

✅ **60 FPS** - Smooth screen mirroring  
✅ **Encrypted** - Secure over local network  
✅ **No App Install** - Just Safari on your phone  
✅ **One QR Scan** - Fast connection  
✅ **Full Screen or Window** - Your choice
